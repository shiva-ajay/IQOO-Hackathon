## microwave_not_heating (diy)

| Line | Agreeing sources |
|---|---|
| Safety: never open the outer casing; high voltage inside | LG p.4 (don't adjust or repair anything behind a cover; "a high-voltage and a high electrical-current piece of equipment"; LG service only); Samsung p.3 (hazardous for anyone but a competent person to remove a cover); IFB p.5 (cover removal: send it to the service centre); Panasonic p.7 ("DO NOT attempt to remove the outer casing") |
| 1 Plug fully in, socket switch on | IFB p.7 (plugged in securely; unplug, wait 10 s, plug in again); LG p.32 (plug in power cord); Panasonic p.45 (plugged in securely); Samsung p.24 ("make sure power is supplied") |
| 2 Breaker or fuse not tripped; test the socket with another appliance | IFB p.7 (blown fuse or tripped breaker; test the outlet with another appliance); Panasonic p.45 (reset the breaker or replace the fuse; plug another appliance in); LG p.32 (fuse or breaker) |
| 2 Caution: breaker keeps tripping → electrician | LG p.32 (circuit overload: have it corrected by a qualified electrician; GFCI keeps tripping: contact an electrician) |
| 3 Wipe food off the door edges, close the door firmly | IFB p.7 (door securely closed, engaging the door lock) and p.3 (no soil or cleaner residue on sealing surfaces); LG p.32 (close oven door); Samsung p.24 (door open; door safety mechanisms covered in foreign matter → remove it) |
| 4 Lock sign on the display = child lock; your manual shows how to unlock | Samsung p.24 (buttons can't be pressed: child lock activated → deactivate); LG p.18 ("Loc" in the display); IFB p.14 (lock indication); Panasonic p.17 ("L" in the display). The unlock method **differs** (LG and IFB hold Stop about 3 s, Panasonic press Stop/Reset 3 times, Samsung its Child Lock button, p.21), so the step says "check your manual" |
| 5 Set a cooking time, press Start | LG p.32 (cooking time not set → set it); IFB p.7 (control panel programmed, timer set); Samsung p.24 (after opening the door, press START again) |
| 5 Caution: never run it empty | LG p.31 ("never run it empty") and p.32; IFB p.3; Samsung p.25 (put food in); Panasonic p.12 (must not be operated without food) |
| 6 Still won't start or heat: technician, don't repair it | IFB p.7 ("contact a qualified technician. Do not try to adjust or repair the oven yourself"); Panasonic p.45 (contact an authorised service centre); Samsung p.26 |

- **Dropped:** the "cup of water for 1-2 minutes" heating test (Samsung p.25 only); Eco / power-save display modes
  (Samsung only); interior light and clock problems (not "won't heat").
- **Escalation:** "keeps tripping" (LG p.32), "burning smell", "sparks inside", "smoke coming out", "door damaged"
  (see the danger entry).

## microwave_sparks (caution)

| Line | Agreeing sources |
|---|---|
| Safety: press Stop straight away | Samsung sparking page ("immediately stop using the microwave if you notice sparks"). Every manual's Stop/Cancel/Reset button does this (LG, IFB, Samsung, Panasonic) |
| Safety: smoke or flames → keep the door shut and unplug | IFB p.4-5; LG p.6; Samsung p.4; Panasonic p.5 (all: switch off or unplug and keep the door closed to stifle flames) |
| 1 Take out foil, metal spoons, twist ties, metal containers | Samsung p.25 (sparks: metal containers) and sparking page (foil, twist ties, metal utensils); LG p.5 (twist ties; metallic containers "may arc"); IFB p.4, p.8 (twist ties; metal utensils; foil at least 1 inch from the walls); Panasonic p.7 (metallic containers: "Sparking will occur") |
| 1 Caution: dish and food may be hot, use oven gloves | IFB p.5 ("use gloves if you remove any heated food"); LG p.28 (dishes may be hot); Samsung p.19 (oven gloves) |
| 2 No gold or silver rims or painted patterns | LG p.5 (gold or silver inlays) and p.28 (painted decoration may contain metal); Samsung sparking page (gold paint); IFB p.8 (metallic trim); Panasonic p.7 (metallic trim) |
| 3 Glass tray and roller ring sitting properly | Samsung sparking page (turntable properly placed) and p.26 (roller ring in place); IFB p.6 (don't operate without the glass tray, roller support and shaft in place); Panasonic p.12 |
| 4 Never run it empty | LG p.32 ("Arcing or sparking: ... operated the oven when it is empty") and p.31; IFB p.3; Samsung p.25; Panasonic p.12 |
| 5 Still sparks with nothing metal inside: stop, technician | Samsung sparking page (if it continues after removing metal, don't repair it yourself, contact service); IFB p.7 (not fixed by the checks: qualified technician); LG p.4 (repairs by the service centre only) |

- **Dropped:** food debris with metal particles (a search summary of the Samsung page mentioned it, the page itself
  didn't); recycled paper (IFB p.8, LG, Panasonic agree, but it's rare; left for a follow-up question).

## microwave_internal_danger (call_technician)

- **Smoke, burning smell, strange noise:** Samsung p.5 ("if the oven generates a strange noise, a burning smell, or smoke
  is emitted, unplug the power plug immediately and contact your nearest service center"); smoke/fire: IFB p.4-5, LG p.6,
  Samsung p.4, Panasonic p.5 (keep the door closed to stifle flames).
- **Damaged door, hinges, seals, glass:** LG p.4 and Samsung p.3 ("if the door or door seals are damaged, the oven must
  not be operated until it has been repaired by a competent person"); IFB p.3, p.5-6; Samsung p.7 (door must not be
  bent, hinges not broken or loose).
- **Anything inside the casing** (capacitor, magnetron, fuse, lamp, power cord): LG p.4 (high voltage; LG service only);
  Samsung p.3; IFB p.3, p.5 (power cord and lamp changes by the service centre); Panasonic p.7 (lamp by a trained service
  technician, don't remove the casing). The high-voltage capacitor is why "never open the casing" is in the spoken line.
- **Meaning:** stop, keep the door shut, unplug, technician, never open the casing: the four manuals above.
